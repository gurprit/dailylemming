How to play

Start the run. Tap the moving green-haired creature to assign a builder. Each builder lays a rising staircase. Press Space to pause, period (.) to advance one frame, or R to restart. The ghost staircase appears while paused so you can plan. Larger touch targets work on phones too.

About this experiment

Real Lix source, tiles, colour-masked terrain and collisions are loaded. Terrain and triggers are imported from Lix; movement is closer to the original 15-updates-per-second physics, but has not been verified against a genuine Lix replay. Does not affect your daily streak. Run npm run lix:vendor:first locally to cache artwork; published builds include it.
