How to play

Press Start. Choose a skill, then tap a grounded Lix to assign it. Use Undo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z) to try a different moment, and pause whenever you need more time to aim. Use Builder, Basher, Digger, Miner, Blocker, Walker, Jumper, Climber, Floater and timed Exploder as the level requires. Exploders keep walking with a five-second countdown before bursting into flying pixels and a destructible crater. These community maps are still compatibility previews, not guaranteed-winnable daily challenges.

Drop into the adventure

These are real multi-skill maps from the Lix Lovely community pack, not tutorials. Everyone receives the same Mystery at each UTC daily reset. The community levels use adapted browser physics and are not yet certified solvable; the separate Verified Daily Challenge is still the place for reliable results and streaks. Original map authors are credited below the game. Nothing here uses commercial Lemmings assets.
